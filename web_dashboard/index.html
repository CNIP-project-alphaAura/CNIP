🔄 TCP vs UDP

TCP (Reliable)

LMS & Exams
  • ✅ 100% Delivery: Retransmits if lost.
  • ✅ Flow Control: Slows down under congestion.
  • ⚠️ Slight Delay: Waits for ACKs.

UDP (Fast Streaming)

Video & Classes
  • ⚡ Low Delay: Sends immediately.
  • 📺 Smooth Video: No pause on packet drop.
  • ⚠️ No Retransmit: Lost packets are gone.
